# Frontier research

Use source territories to resolve a decision-space gap, not as mandatory idea categories.

## Territories

- **Primary:** official documentation, standards, research, books, and original cases for mechanisms, boundaries, and decision facts.
- **Practice:** practitioner reports, communities, video, social sources, and failures for friction and execution leads; unsupported claims remain hypotheses.
- **Ready routes:** products, templates, OSS, experts, benefits, partners, delegation, reuse, and manual paths before custom build.
- **Transfer:** search by target function, operating constraint, and failure mode—not by a fashionable source domain.
- **Failure/boundary:** non-transfer, subtraction, prevention versus containment/recovery, ownership, scope, incentives, and combinations.

For open or stuck tasks preserve two kinds of independence:

1. one anchor-blind pass receives the neutral problem frame, constraints, and failed attempts but no candidate prose;
2. gap-directed passes receive occupied mechanism keys, forbidden near-duplicates, and one decision-relevant frontier gap.

Return compact candidates as `mechanism_key | route_key | causal chain | practical first action | evidence lead | falsifier`. A negative result is valid evidence of saturation. Use [solution operators](solution-operators.md) only when the residual frontier stalls; they are internal transformations, never output labels.

Stop a territory when another batch cannot produce a route that clears constraints and changes the top set, eligibility, or material risk.
