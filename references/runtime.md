# Runtime and degradation

Use host capabilities, never named models. Keep the chair on the current capable model and use the cheapest worker adequate for a bounded lane. Do not increase calls merely to demonstrate breadth.

When workers exist, use at most three parallel first-wave calls:

- one anchor-blind counter-map;
- up to two distinct high-value frontier gaps.

Give each a neutral frame, hard constraints, source territory, compact schema, output cap, and read-only/no-side-effect/no-recursion rules. Do not share candidate prose. The chair merges by causal mechanism and ranks globally.

Run at most one additional worker or chair pass. Before it, record a compact shell contract: `gap | result that would change the decision | cheapest search/probe | stop condition`. Skip the pass when no result could clear the utility floor or change eligibility, top-set membership, or material risk. Without workers, perform short isolated chair passes. Without browsing, separate inference from fact and provide direct verification routes. Tool or worker failure is disclosed, not simulated.

Stop when the last meaningful pass adds no usable mechanism, conditional winner, material risk/recovery change, or rank-changing evidence.
