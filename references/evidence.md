# Evidence and transfer

Evidence strength and route fit are separate. Label only decision-critical claims: `[strength/fit/status/origin]`.

- Strength: `A` direct/current exact primary; `B` partial primary; `C` credible secondary/adjacent case; `D` practitioner/vendor lead; `I` inference.
- Fit: `exact | adjacent | uncertain`.
- Status: `confirmed | available/bookable | supported-unconfirmed | unknown | unavailable`.
- Origin: `primary | research | case | practitioner | transfer`.

For each leading conditional winner keep 1–3 load-bearing claims:

`claim | type | support/status | decision threshold | cheapest disconfirmer | affected route/rank`

Include a claim only if refuting it could remove or reorder a top route, change a conditional winner, or expose material risk. Claim types include existence/current availability, causal effect, local feasibility, economics/resources, and risk/reversibility. If an effect is unsupported, make it a bounded experiment with an observable criterion.

Scope labels to atomic claims. For time-sensitive facts add `checked: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM timezone`. Existence never proves inventory, price, acceptance, delivery, or bookability. Correlated citations count once. Separate observation from predicted intervention effect and seek one independent disconfirmer for each leading dependency when feasible.

Practice, video, and social sources are leads, not rank overrides. A transfer survives only as `source relation → target relation → causal lever → changed assumption → boundary → cheapest local falsifier`. Distant origin earns no bonus. Prefer the route only if its fit, utility, and evidence survive the ordinary gate.
