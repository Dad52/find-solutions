# Residual frontier prompts

Read only when an open or repeatedly stuck task has fewer than five viable mechanisms after ordinary gating and a plausible decision-relevant constraint world remains uncovered. Apply one mutation to the strongest gap, then return to the ordinary gates. These are internal transformations; never return their names as solutions.

- Move the intervention upstream, downstream, or to the governing boundary.
- Change the causal lever, owner/access, economics, horizon, or recovery model.
- Invert a credible failure chain: remove the trigger, break propagation, contain damage, detect earlier, or recover cheaply.
- Test an extreme constraint or opposite assumption to expose leverage, then return to the real constraints.
- Replace expensive prevention with reversibility, graceful degradation, transferred exposure, or a leading signal.
- Search another domain by equivalent function, constraint, and failure relation; require a local falsifier.

Keep a mutation only when it clears the ordinary utility and evidence gates and changes a mechanism key, conditional winner, or material fallback. Stop rather than fill empty cells with implausible novelty.
